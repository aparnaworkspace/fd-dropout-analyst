# Indian Retail Investor Behavior — FD Context

## First-Time Investor Profile
- Age: typically 22-35, salaried, urban or semi-urban
- Primary concern: safety of capital over returns
- Decision trigger: received first salary, received bonus, or family advice
- Common behavior: compares 5-8 options before deciding, reads reviews
- Drop-off trigger: overwhelmed by jargon, fears making wrong choice
- Reactivation: peer recommendation or simplified comparison tool works best

## Tier 2 / Tier 3 City Investor
- Cities: Gorakhpur, Patna, Lucknow, Bhopal, Surat, Coimbatore, Nagpur
- Higher trust deficit with digital platforms vs branch banking
- Prefers Hindi or regional language interfaces
- Higher sensitivity to DICGC insurance status of bank
- Often first-generation digital investor
- Reactivation: WhatsApp nudge in vernacular language is most effective

## Comparison Paralysis Pattern
- Triggered when user compares 4+ banks across 2+ sessions
- User is interested but cognitively overloaded
- Has not made a decision within 7 days of first comparison session
- Most effective intervention: "Best pick for your goal" recommendation
  that removes the need to decide independently

## Silent Churner Profile
- Visited platform, browsed rates, did not engage with KYC
- Sessions under 5 minutes, low scroll depth
- Often browsing casually or testing the platform
- Low reactivation rate — requires strong incentive or trigger event
- Best reactivation: rate increase notification or festive season offer

## Investment Amount Patterns
- Rs 10,000 - Rs 25,000: Young first-timers, testing the platform
- Rs 50,000 - Rs 1,00,000: Core retail segment, high intent
- Rs 2,00,000+: High-value users, higher due diligence, slower to convert
