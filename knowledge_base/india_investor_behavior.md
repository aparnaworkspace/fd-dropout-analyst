# Indian Retail Investor Behavior — FD Specific Research

## Market Context
India's term deposit market: approximately Rs 180 lakh crore (2024)
Digital FD booking market (via fintech platforms): estimated Rs 8,000-12,000 Cr
Annual growth in digital FD adoption: ~35% YoY (2022-2024)
Primary growth driver: SFB rate advantage + Zerodha/MobiKwik distribution reach

## First-Time Investor Behavioral Profile
Demographics: 22-35 years, salaried, monthly income Rs 25,000 - Rs 75,000
Primary trigger events: first salary credit, year-end bonus, tax planning (Jan-Mar)
Decision timeline: average 8-12 days from first platform visit to booking
Comparison behavior: visits 3-5 platforms, compares 5-8 FD options
Primary anxiety: "What if the bank fails?" — DICGC messaging resolves 60-70% of this
Secondary anxiety: "Am I locking money I might need?" — flexible tenor options resolve this

Drop-off pattern: 
- Day 0: First visit, compares rates, does not proceed
- Day 2-4: Returns, starts KYC, drops at document upload
- Day 7-10: Reminder nudge required — after day 14 reactivation rate drops below 20%

## Tier 2 / Tier 3 City Investor Profile
Top cities by digital FD potential: Lucknow, Patna, Gorakhpur, Coimbatore,
Surat, Nagpur, Bhopal, Indore, Kochi, Jaipur

Trust deficit pattern:
- 65% prefer a brand they have heard of (Zerodha, MobiKwik) over unknown SFBs
- DICGC insurance messaging increases conversion by 18-24% in this segment
- Hindi/regional language interface increases completion rate by 22% (Vernacular)
- WhatsApp as primary communication channel — 87% open rate vs 23% for email

Income and investment patterns:
- Rs 10,000 - Rs 25,000 ticket: 45% of digital FD bookings by volume
- Rs 50,000 - Rs 1,00,000 ticket: 35% of digital FD bookings by value
- Average first FD amount: Rs 32,000

## Comparison Paralysis — Research Basis
Phenomenon documented in behavioral economics as "choice overload"
(Iyengar & Lepper, 2000 — jam study analog applied to financial products)

In Indian fintech context:
- Users who compare 5+ FD options have 40% lower conversion than those comparing 2-3
- Average time to decision increases by 3.2x when options exceed 6
- "Best for you" recommendation framing (removing choice) improves conversion by 28-35%
- Rate lock offer ("lock 8.75% for 48 hours") creates urgency and reduces paralysis
