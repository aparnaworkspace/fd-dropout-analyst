# Intervention Playbook — FD Dropout Cohorts

## KYC Dropout
**Root Cause**: Technical or process friction during document submission
**Urgency**: High — user has high intent, just stuck
**Recommended Interventions**:
1. Trigger WhatsApp message within 2 hours of KYC abandonment with
   step-by-step guide for the specific step they dropped at
2. Offer Video KYC callback scheduling — removes async friction
3. In-app banner: "Complete your KYC in 3 steps — we'll guide you"
4. If support query raised: assign a human agent within 24 hours
**Expected Reactivation Rate**: 35-45% with timely intervention

## Comparison Paralysis
**Root Cause**: Too many options, cognitive overload, no clear recommendation
**Urgency**: Medium — user is interested but cooling down
**Recommended Interventions**:
1. "Best FD for you" recommendation card based on their viewed tenor and amount
2. Rate lock offer: "Lock 8.75% p.a. from AU SFB for 48 hours"
3. Social proof nudge: "2,400 users booked this FD this week"
4. Simplify display to show only top 3 options matching their behavior
**Expected Reactivation Rate**: 25-35% within 7 days

## Overwhelmed First-Timer
**Root Cause**: Lack of financial literacy, fear of making wrong decision
**Urgency**: Medium — emotionally engaged but needs reassurance
**Recommended Interventions**:
1. Trigger onboarding flow: "Your first FD — a 2-minute guide"
2. DICGC insurance explainer: "Your money is insured up to Rs 5 lakh"
3. Vernacular language switch prompt if Tier 2 city detected
4. Peer story: "Rahul from Lucknow started with Rs 10,000 — here's his story"
**Expected Reactivation Rate**: 30-40% with educational nudge

## Silent Churner
**Root Cause**: Low intent visit, no strong trigger to convert
**Urgency**: Low — do not over-invest in reactivation
**Recommended Interventions**:
1. Rate change notification: "FD rates just increased — lock in now"
2. Festive season or year-end bonus nudge
3. Retargeting with simplified single-option offer
4. Do not trigger aggressive outreach — increases uninstall risk
**Expected Reactivation Rate**: 10-15% — focus resources on other cohorts
