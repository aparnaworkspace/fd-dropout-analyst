# KYC Process for FD Booking in India

KYC (Know Your Customer) is mandatory for all FD bookings in India as per RBI
guidelines. It is the single largest source of drop-off in digital FD journeys.

## Documents Required
- PAN Card (mandatory)
- Aadhaar Card (address proof)
- Cancelled cheque or bank statement (for payout account linking)
- Selfie / live photo (for video KYC)

## Types of KYC
- **Full KYC**: In-person or Video KYC — required for accounts above Rs 50,000
- **OTP-based Aadhaar eKYC**: For amounts below Rs 50,000, faster but limited
- **Video KYC (V-CIP)**: RBI-approved remote process — takes 5-10 minutes live

## Common Friction Points
1. Document upload failures on slow mobile connections
2. Name mismatch between PAN and Aadhaar
3. Video KYC agent unavailability during peak hours
4. Users unaware they need a cancelled cheque
5. Selfie liveness detection failures on older devices

## Tier 2/3 City Specific Issues
Users from smaller cities often face:
- Slower internet causing upload timeouts
- Older Android devices with poor camera quality for liveness checks
- Less familiarity with video KYC process
- Higher likelihood of name discrepancies in legacy documents

## Impact on Conversion
KYC abandonment accounts for 35-45% of total FD booking drop-offs in India's
digital fintech platforms. Users who raise support queries during KYC have a
3x higher churn rate than those who complete it without friction.
