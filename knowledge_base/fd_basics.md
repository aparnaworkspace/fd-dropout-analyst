# Fixed Deposits in India — Core Concepts

A Fixed Deposit (FD) is a financial instrument offered by banks and NBFCs where
a customer deposits a lump sum for a fixed tenor at a predetermined interest rate.

## Key Terms
- **Tenor**: Duration of the FD, typically 7 days to 10 years
- **Interest Rate (p.a.)**: Annual interest rate paid on the principal
- **Maturity Amount**: Principal + interest earned at end of tenor
- **TDS**: Tax Deducted at Source on FD interest above Rs 40,000/year
- **Premature Withdrawal**: Breaking FD before maturity — usually incurs a penalty
  of 0.5% to 1% reduction in interest rate

## Small Finance Banks (SFBs)
SFBs like Suryoday, ESAF, Ujjivan, AU, Jana, and Equitas offer FD rates
significantly higher than large banks — typically 7.5% to 9.5% p.a. vs 6.5% to
7.5% from PSU banks. Deposits up to Rs 5 lakh are insured under DICGC.

## Why Users Choose FDs
- Guaranteed returns, no market risk
- Higher rates from SFBs vs savings accounts
- Short tenors available (3-6 months) for parking surplus funds
- Senior citizens get additional 0.25% to 0.5% rate benefit

## Common Drop-off Points
1. During bank comparison — too many options cause paralysis
2. During KYC — document upload friction
3. Post-comparison — user goes cold after researching but not deciding
4. At amount entry — uncertainty about how much to invest
