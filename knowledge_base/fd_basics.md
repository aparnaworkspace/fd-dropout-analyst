# Fixed Deposits in India — Core Concepts

## What is a Fixed Deposit
A Fixed Deposit (FD) is a term deposit offered by scheduled commercial banks,
small finance banks (SFBs), and NBFCs where a customer deposits a lump sum for
a fixed tenor at a locked interest rate. Unlike mutual funds or equities, FDs
carry no market risk — the return is contractually guaranteed.

## Interest Rate Landscape (2024-2025)
Large PSU banks (SBI, PNB, Bank of Baroda): 6.50% - 7.25% p.a.
Private banks (HDFC, ICICI, Axis): 7.00% - 7.75% p.a.
Small Finance Banks: 7.50% - 9.50% p.a.

Key SFB rates as of 2024:
- Suryoday SFB: up to 9.51% p.a. (444 days tenor)
- ESAF SFB: up to 8.75% p.a. (2 years)
- Ujjivan SFB: up to 8.25% p.a. (12 months)
- AU SFB: up to 8.00% p.a. (18 months)
- Jana SFB: up to 8.50% p.a. (1 year)
- Equitas SFB: up to 8.25% p.a. (888 days)

## DICGC Insurance — Critical for Tier 2/3 Trust
All deposits in scheduled banks including SFBs are insured by the Deposit
Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC) up to Rs 5,00,000 per
depositor per bank. This includes both principal and interest.

This is the single most effective trust signal for first-time investors from
Tier 2/3 cities — more effective than interest rate comparisons.
RBI Circular: DICGC Act 1961, amended 2021 (increased from Rs 1L to Rs 5L).

## Tenor and Liquidity
- Minimum tenor: 7 days (most banks)
- Maximum tenor: 10 years
- Premature withdrawal penalty: typically 0.5% to 1.0% interest rate reduction
- Tax on interest: TDS deducted if interest exceeds Rs 40,000/year (Rs 50,000
  for senior citizens) under Section 194A of Income Tax Act

## Why SFBs Dominate Digital FD Platforms
SFBs like Suryoday and ESAF offer 150-250 bps higher rates than PSU banks.
On a Rs 1,00,000 deposit for 12 months, this translates to Rs 1,500 - Rs 2,500
additional interest — a meaningful difference for the Rs 25,000 - Rs 1,00,000
segment that dominates digital FD platforms.
